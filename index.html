InterpretationNormalized difference indices are dimensionless. They are only as meaningful as the input reflectance, atmospheric correction, masks and intended algorithm. NDCI is a proxy in some waters, not a universally calibrated chlorophyll concentration.
OLCI study checklist
Before deriving water-quality products
01 · Product levelPrefer the appropriate Level-2 product for the variable.
02 · Quality flagsMask clouds, invalid pixels, high glint and atmospheric failures.
03 · Coastal effectsAccount for shallow bottom, adjacency and highly turbid water.
04 · ValidationCompare with field/lab samples and a documented algorithm.
Band reference

OLCI bands around 665 nm (red), 708 nm (red-edge) and 865 nm (near-infrared) can support different vegetation and aquatic research methods. Check exact band definitions and processing level in mission documentation.

Research tools / Sample calculations

Spectral lab

Calculate normalized-difference indices from manually entered reflectance samples and document a repeatable analysis workflow. The app does not sample raster pixels.

Reflectance inputs
Unitless surface reflectance from 0–1
Manual inputs
Formulas

NDVI = (NIR − Red)/(NIR + Red). NDWI (McFeeters-style) = (Green − NIR)/(Green + NIR). NDCI = (Red-edge − Red)/(Red-edge + Red). These are index formulas only; water-quality interpretation and product algorithm validation are separate.

Processing workflow
For a defensible research analysis
01
Choose an appropriate Level-2 product

OLCI water products for ocean-colour research; land products for vegetation/land studies.

02
Mask invalid observations

Respect cloud, atmospheric correction, glint, land/water and quality flags.

03
Reproject and align

Confirm geolocation and resolution before comparing multiple acquisitions.

04
Validate and record provenance

Save product ID, processing baseline, bands, units, algorithm and any field validation.

No pixel samplingThis browser-only calculator does not download NetCDF products, read raster values, apply quality flags or calculate spatial summaries from a selected scene.
